About the Raw Materials Experience

Raw Materials brings a mindful, wellness-based approach to beverage consumption. Each beverage is crafted with clean ingredients that cultivate a symphony of taste that demands your attention.

We encourage you to challenge your consumption of coffee and alcohol and replace it with this much more delightful flavor experience.

a note from our founder

I stopped drinking coffee in 2020 and replaced it with tea. My love of tea became a conversation starter, and something I'm known for. In 2026 I decided to launch my own pop-up café, to bring my love of tea on the go and share my unique flavor explorations with more people.

I hope you and your friends will join me in this journey to discover new flavors and ignite new sensations.

In 2020 I stopped drinking coffee and started drinking tea. I began with matcha and chai lattes, and I spent that year looking for the best chai in cafés and posting reviews to my Instagram and Snapchat stories. Then I started making matcha at home, with different powders, milks, and sugars.

In 2024 I went to Tokyo for the first time and bought matcha from a local shop. The first time I made tea with it, I took a sip before adding milk or sugar. My eyes widened. I gasped. It was more delicious than anything I had known before. At this point, I stopped going to cafés because I couldn't find a drink better than what I could make at home.

By 2025, I was working from home and drinking tea almost every hour. I would consider myself a tea mixologist with the number of recipes I have tested and repeated. My most surprising combo: tea and sparkling water. This is the recipe that set my tea consumption apart from anyone else I know.

Raw Materials began in 2026 when I was drinking so much tea that I needed a better way to buy it. I'm now a wholesale partner with Spirit Tea, which works directly with farmers. Every day I learn a little bit more about tea and its origins so I can bring that knowledge to my customers.

Raw Materials is a pop-up café where I bring my love of tea on the go. Local community is at the center of everything we do. Our favorite partners are local business owners, wellness studios, and mission-driven people. We are working toward a market stand at farmers markets around Atlanta in spring 2027.
